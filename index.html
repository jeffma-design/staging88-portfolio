How I lead

Brand strategy and design leadership, built for high-stakes rooms

The work below spans two disciplines that, in practice, are one job: building a brand’s point of view, and building the team and system that expresses it consistently — to executives, regulators, and the public alike.

01

Translate expertise into narrative

I work directly with engineers, policy leads, and security researchers to turn deep technical and legal complexity — AI risk frameworks, data protection regulation, cybersecurity posture — into visuals and stories that land with non-expert audiences.

02

Lead teams and agency partners

I’ve managed embedded designers, contractors, and agency partners across simultaneous high-visibility engagements, setting creative direction while staying accountable for craft, consistency, and deadlines under real pressure.

03

Build for the room it’s presented in

From a boardroom briefing to a public-facing campaign, I design for the specific audience and stakes of the moment — knowing that a slide for the Alphabet Board and a film for millions of users require entirely different instincts, built on the same discipline.
